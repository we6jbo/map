Code guide

Part Example Meaning
Family shortcode 7H Family group or research topic.
Day number 01 June 4, 2026 is day 01. September 1, 2026 is day 90.
Time code 4B 4 hours after 7 AM = 11 AM hour. B = minutes 15–29.
Area 2 Mission Bay area number.
